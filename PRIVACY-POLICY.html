Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

CDRA (cdras.github.io) is a small static website with stream-milestone awards for SETTWOR, run by SETTWOR. This page says what happens to your data when you visit it. Short version: we do not collect any.

What the site does not do

No cookies. No accounts, sign-ups, forms or comments. No analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Nothing is stored in your browser, with one exception: if you pick Light or Dark in the footer, that choice is saved in your browser’s local storage (one word, key cdra-theme) so the page does not switch back. It never leaves your device and is removed when you choose Auto or clear the site data.

What happens in your browser

The pages use one small script of our own (js/tilt.js). It tilts the award card toward the mouse on a computer and lets you refresh the page by pulling down on a touch screen. It runs only in your browser, makes no network requests and sends nothing anywhere. Fonts and pictures come from the site itself, with one exception: the SETTWOR picture is loaded from settwor.github.io, the artist’s own website.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical data when a page is requested (for example your IP address and browser details) and may keep it in its server logs under its own privacy statement. We do not receive these logs and do not use them. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Award cards on other websites

Other websites may show an award card from this site (see how to add one). The card is a small page of this site, loaded by the visitor’s browser from cdras.github.io. It has no script, sets no cookies and stores nothing on the device; the request reaches GitHub Pages like any other page request (see Hosting). Clicking the card opens its award page here.

Links to other sites

The site links to other websites: settwor.github.io, ledgertone.github.io, Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Instagram, Telegram, X (share buttons) and others. When you open one of them, that site’s own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for it.

Search engines

The site is verified in Google Search Console, which shows the owner only general search statistics. No tracking code is added to the pages for this.

Children

The site does not ask anyone for personal information, including children.

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no personal data about visitors, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you still have a question, write to SETTWOR on Telegram or Instagram.

Changes

If the site starts to collect anything, this page will be updated first and the date above will change. The current version of the site is shown in the footer.
